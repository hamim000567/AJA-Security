About Our Company

A London Security Company Trusted Across England

AJA Security Services is a London-based security company providing professional, dependable security for businesses, construction sites, retailers, venues and events in London and across England.

Our approach is simple. We take the time to understand your premises, your risks and your budget, then put the right security in place - whether that's a single officer, a team of door supervisors for a busy venue, regular mobile patrols or round-the-clock guarding.

Operating from our office in central London, we can deploy officers to any city in England and we're available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so help is always just a phone call away.

  • Static guarding, door and event security
  • Construction, retail and corporate security
  • CCTV monitoring, mobile patrols and key holding
  • SIA Approved Contractor, NSI Gold, SSAIB and COP 11 certified
  • One point of contact from quote to deployment
